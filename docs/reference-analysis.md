## Translation to this portfolio

- The profile uses original concentric-circle artwork as an explicitly labeled portrait placeholder. No reference logo, lettering, screenshot, or protected design asset is shipped.
- The archive uses consistent rows, an elevated near-isometric camera, a selected record, front/rear instanced decorative cases, a persistent right-side overview, and small metadata labels.
- A vertical transition links profile and collection. Reading remains available immediately through a text index.
- Four muted project colors come from content, with layered transparent edge geometry rather than bloom or per-record lights.
- The nine-stage jewel-case/disc/player sequence is an original extension of the user's written specification. Neither supplied clip demonstrates the complete tray-loading sequence, so its timing and mechanical layout are designed for this portfolio rather than claimed as a reproduction.
- The revised timeline completes in approximately 3 seconds. Case positioning, lid opening, disc extraction, and player entrance overlap; tray retraction starts as soon as loading completes. There are no deliberate idle gaps. Exact timing is centralized in `motion.ts`.

## Deliberate adaptations

The profile prioritizes education, engineering focus, skills, and public internship information over a long splash screen. Only four records are actual projects; instanced background cases are visual geometry and have no picking behavior. Mobile uses a smaller composition with the overview below it. Reduced motion bypasses the loading sequence. Direct project pages never require WebGL or JavaScript.

## Interaction refinement

The collection at 5 seconds in the first clip and the closing frames of the second show diagonal upper edges. In the portfolio, the top edges recede to the upper right. Projects meet at their narrow edges along the case width. Blank cases continue along the cover normal on both sides. All cases share the same orientation and travel together when the selection moves.

The selected case rises from its current height along a 0.52-second ease-out curve. Nearby cases receive a separate ripple with a distance-dependent delay. Once the lift finishes, the selected case gradually joins the collection's slow travelling wave. The visible collection requests idle frames at 30 Hz. Reduced motion holds a static composition. An entrance wave, pointer movement and dragging also affect the shelf.

The lid starts opening 0.18 seconds into the case movement. The player finishes loading at 3 seconds. Outgoing overview text fades for 160 milliseconds before the incoming project appears.
